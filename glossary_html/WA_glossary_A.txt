							<td valign=top><span class="style18">This

								of survival.<br>
								<b>Behavior:</b> Any change in behavior that is prompted by changing environmental 
								conditions.<br>
								<b>Neurological / Sensory:</b> A reduction in neuronal sensitivity (reduction 
								in firing rate or size of evoked potentials) to repeated stimuli of constant 
								intensity. Sensory adaptation is likely to play a role in some normal mechanisms of body movement in <em>C. elegans</em> (<a href="http://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00657352" target="_blank">Dusenbery, 1980</a>) and has been shown to change responses to volatile odorants (<a href="http://dx.doi.org/10.1111/j.1601-183X.2004.00080.x" target="_blank">Bettinger and McIntire, 2004</a>). </span></td>
