							<td valign=top class="style18">&nbsp;</td>
							<td valign=top class="style18"><p>Mutations causing an<a href="http://www.wormbase.org/species/all/phenotype/WBPhenotype:0000643" target="_blank"> abnormal locomotion phenotype</a> in the animal, which may  include subtle twitching or halting behavior, odd jerky motions, curling or  kinky motions, constant twitching, shrinking and expanding lengthwise without  progress of the body, partial paralysis, or limp or rigid paralysis. Uncoordination is a  very common phenotype among genetic mutations in <em>C. elegans</em>, only some of which  are numbered among the listed <a href="http://www.wormbase.org/resources/gene_class/unc" target="_blank">unc mutants</a>. The affected genes may be expressed either in neurons, muscles or even  in other tissues affecting the structures required for coordinated movement,  including the cuticle (as in <a href="http://www.wormbase.org/resources/gene_class/rol" target="_blank">Rol mutants</a>). Some of these phenotypes are recognizable as morphological traits or  spontaneous defects in movement, while some others are only revealed when the  animal is stimulated to move, perhaps by prodding with a fine wire.</p>

										See <a href="cglossary.htm#coiler" target="_blank">Coiler&nbsp;</a>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<br>
